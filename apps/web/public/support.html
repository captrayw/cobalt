Questions, problems or ideas: write to info@acolorcompany.com.

A session

cobalt opens straight into full-screen colors and soft tones. Swipe up, or tap the arrow at the bottom, for the next page. Stay on a page and it starts to breathe. Tap the × at the top right to end the session and see its summary.

No sound?

The sound starts as cobalt opens. If you hear nothing, turn the volume up with the buttons on the side of the phone. The Settings screen has its own volume slider, and a button that plays a note.

Day and night

In a session, swipe right for night: warm, dim colors with no blue, a little darker page by page. Swipe left for day. The choice is kept for later sessions, and Settings has it too, under Screen and color.

Moving to a new phone

On the Memory screen, Export saves one file with your memory, sessions and settings. On the new phone, Import on the same screen reads it back.

Privacy

cobalt collects nothing and keeps everything on your device. The privacy policy has the details.
